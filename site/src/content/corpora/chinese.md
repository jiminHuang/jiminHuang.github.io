This page describes tools and resources developed at NaCTeM to aid with the processing of biomedical text in the Chinese language. NaCTeM's TerMine service has been adapted to handle the extraction of terms from Chinese biomedical text. This adaptation consists of the sequential application of two pre-processing steps prior to running the existing TerMine service.
