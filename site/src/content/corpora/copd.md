The COPD corpus is a semantically annotated corpus, focussed on phenotypic information, consisting of 30 full-text articles. The corpus has been manually annotated with named entities, using a fine-grained annotation scheme, which aims to capture detailed information about COPD phenotypes. In particular, the annotations may be "nested" within each other.
