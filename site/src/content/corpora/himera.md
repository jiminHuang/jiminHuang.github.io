The HIMERA annotated corpus contains a set of published historical medical documents that has been manually annotated with semantic information that relevant to the study of medical history and public health. Specifically, annotations correspond to seven different entity types and two different event types (which encode relationships amongst entities), chosen based on extensive discussions with medical historians.
