The popularity of social media in recent years has promoted the spread of fake news. Detecting fake news on social media is challenging, as pieces of fake news pieces are intentionally written to mislead consumers, which means that it is often not possible to spot fake news from news content itself. For this reason, social context based detection methods, which attempt to model the spreading patterns of fake news by utilising the collective wisdom from users on social media, have been attracting increasing attention.
