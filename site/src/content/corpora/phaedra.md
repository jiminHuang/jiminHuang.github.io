The PHAEDRA corpus is a semantically annotated corpus for pharmacovigilence (PV), consisting of 597 MEDLINE abstracts. Its fine-grained, multiple levels of annotation, added by domain-experts, make it a unique resource within the field, and aim to encourage the development/adaption of novel machine learning tools for extracting PV-related information from text. It is intended that such tools will lead to novel means of supporting curators to efficiently increase the coverage, consistency and completeness of the information in PV resources.
