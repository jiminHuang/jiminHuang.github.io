Owing to the highly technical nature of biomedical documents, the ease with which people can understand their content varies according to their level of domain knowledge. While existing biomedical document summarization systems are generally only able to produce highly technical summaries, it would be desirable for them also to be able to produce plain language summaries (PLSs) that can be understood by lay people.
