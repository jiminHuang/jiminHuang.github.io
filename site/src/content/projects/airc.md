Prof. Sophia Ananiadou, director of NaCTeM and Prof. Angelo Cangelosi's Cognitive Robotics Lab at Manchester are collaborating with the Artificial Intelligence Research Center (AIRC) , in Tokyo, Japan, to carry out fundamental NLP and text mining research. AIRC is directed by Prof. Jun'ichi Tsujii , who also holds the position of Professor of Text Mining at the University of Manchester, and is NaCTeM's scientific advisor.
