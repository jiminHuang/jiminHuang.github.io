The aim of this pilot project is to analyse, structure and visualise BBC news available on the Web according to a user's query using advanced text mining techniques. The major outcomes will include a web demonstrator of two concept clustering tools and also presentations to identified sets of potential users within New Media & Technology, News and BBC Monitoring, and to BBC Research/Technology Group. Duration: July - December 2007 Principal Investigator: Sophia Ananiadou Research Associate: Bria
