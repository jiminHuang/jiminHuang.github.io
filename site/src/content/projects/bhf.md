In partnership with the **British Heart Foundation**, NaCTeM is building the
tooling that lets the BHF research office read its portfolio at the scale of
the published literature: surfacing emerging cardiovascular topics, mapping
who funds what, and tracking the downstream impact of BHF-funded papers
across the field.
