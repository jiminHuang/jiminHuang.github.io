The collaborative project aims to advance the means by which Philippine biodiversity information is being collected and published, through the construction of an online knowledge repository whose content will be semi-automatically curated by text mining-based analytics. Currently, information on Philippine biodiversity is largely fragmented due to the siloed formats in which different local institutions store their data.
