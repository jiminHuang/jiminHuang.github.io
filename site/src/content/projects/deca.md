Motivation Manually searching pieces of information in the ocean of research papers can be very difficult and time-consuming. This task becomes even more demanding if one performs searches on biomedical texts, which tend to involve large numbers of named entities in specialised domains. In addition, biologists are often interested in knowing certain types of associations between the entities, such as interactions between proteins and relations between symptoms and diseases.
