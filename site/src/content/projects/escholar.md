Manchester eScholar is a search facility at the University of Manchester that gives researchers access to scholarly work produced by individuals associated with the university. NaCTeM, in collaboration with the University of Manchester Library (UML) , will enrich the current faceted search capabilities of eScholar by customising, adapting and combining existing text mining tools and algorithms to foster the discovery of interdisciplinary links.
