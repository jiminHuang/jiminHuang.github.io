The Lloyds HSE project is under the umbrella of the £10 million Discovering Safety programme funded by Lloyd's Register Foundation. Central to the programme is the development of new technologies to analyse data and aggregate data from sources worldwide, the key output being new learning to help prevent future accidents occurring.
