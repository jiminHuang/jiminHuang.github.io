META-NET is a Network of Excellence dedicated to fostering the technological foundations of a multilingual European information society. Language Technologies allow a number of major challenges to be overcome: enabling communication and cooperation across languages, securing users of any language equal access to information and knowledge, building upon and advancing functionalities of networked information technology.
