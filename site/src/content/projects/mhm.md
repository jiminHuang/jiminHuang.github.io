This project will demonstrate the potential of text mining tools to helping researchers from multiple disciplines to discover and extract information automatically from medical historical archives. It brings together the strengths of two teams at the University of Manchester: The National Centre for Text Mining (NaCTeM), with its proven track record of developing effective text mining tools operating in a variety of domains. The Centre for the History of Science, Technology and Medicine (CHSTM)
