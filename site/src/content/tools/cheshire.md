Cheshire 3 is the **latest incarnation of the Cheshire IR system** originally developed at UC Berkeley. It indexes structured XML, supports probabilistic and Boolean retrieval, and scales to multi-terabyte collections. NaCTeM uses it as the indexing backbone of several services.
