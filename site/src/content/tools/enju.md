Enju is a **wide-coverage HPSG parser** developed at the University of Tokyo and distributed with NaCTeM tooling. It produces phrase structure plus predicate-argument relations — the input format that MEDIE indexes for semantic search.

Trained on the Penn Treebank, it has been adapted to biomedical text and is benchmarked against state-of-the-art parsers on the GENIA corpus.
