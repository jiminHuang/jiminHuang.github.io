FACTA+ ("**Fa**ster **C**oncept-**T**o-**A**ssociation") returns a ranked list of concepts — genes, diseases, drugs, enzymes, compounds, symptoms — associated with a user query, drawn from co-occurrence patterns across MEDLINE.

Type "p53" and FACTA+ surfaces the most strongly associated diseases and pathways; type "diabetes" and get associated genes and drugs.
