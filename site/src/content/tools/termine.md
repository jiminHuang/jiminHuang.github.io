TerMine identifies the **technical terminology** of a document collection using the C-value algorithm, which combines linguistic filters with statistical measures of termhood. Upload a corpus and get back a ranked list of candidate multi-word terms.

It is one of NaCTeM's longest-running services, used across biomedicine, chemistry, social science, and the humanities to bootstrap domain glossaries.
