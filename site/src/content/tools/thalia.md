Thalia is a **semantic search engine over PubMed**. It pre-annotates the full biomedical literature with eight entity types — chemicals, diseases, drugs, genes, mutations, methods, organisms, and metabolites — and lets users query by entity rather than only by keyword.

You can ask, for example, for papers that mention *gene X* and *disease Y* but not necessarily by the exact strings you remember.
