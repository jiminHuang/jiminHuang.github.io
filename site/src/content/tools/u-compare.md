U-Compare lets researchers **compare NLP components side-by-side** on the same input. Drag in two parsers, two taggers, or two whole pipelines; U-Compare runs them in parallel, aligns the outputs, and produces evaluation reports.

The system is built around the UIMA component model, so any UIMA-wrapped tool can be slotted in. Originally developed jointly with the University of Tokyo, U-Compare is widely used in shared-task benchmarking.
